## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our software seriously. If you have found a vulnerability, please report it to us as quickly as possible.

### How to Report

Please report security vulnerabilities by emailing [INSERT EMAIL ADDRESS]. We will acknowledge your email within 48 hours.

### What to Include

* Detailed steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
* The version of the software you were using.
* Any relevant configuration details.

### Our Process

1. We will investigate the report.
2. We will determine the impact of the vulnerability.
3. We will develop a fix.
4. We will release a patch.
5. We will announce the vulnerability and fix.

Thank you for helping keep our community safe!
